================================================================================
PROJECT CONTEXT: CoF2 DFT+U Simulation (Quantum ESPRESSO)
================================================================================

MATERIAL: Cobalt Difluoride (CoF2)
GOAL: Study altermagnetic properties of CoF2 using DFT+U (PBEsol functional),
      including structural relaxation, Hubbard U determination, magnetic phase
      comparison, spin-orbit coupling (SOC), and Wannier90 band interpolation.

REFERENCE LINKS:
  - PRB paper: https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.74.195118
  - Altermagnet DB: https://altermagnets.anyterial.se/material?id=anyt:am-1-0008
  - QE input generator: https://qeinputgenerator.materialscloud.io/

CRYSTAL STRUCTURE:
  - Space group: tetragonal (ibrav=6, P4_2/mnm, rutile-type)
  - Experimental lattice: a = 4.6435 Å, c = 3.1992 Å (c/a ≈ 0.6890)
  - 6 atoms/cell: 2x Co (Co1 spin-up, Co2 spin-down), 4x F
  - Co1 at (0,0,0), Co2 at (0.5,0.5,0.5) — antiferromagnetic sublattices
  - F at fractional positions ~(0.196, 0.804, 0.5) and symmetry equivalents
  - Pseudopotentials:
      Co: co_pbesol_v1.2.uspp.F.UPF (USPP, non-relativistic, for collinear)
          Co.pbesol-spn-kjpaw_psl.0.3.1.UPF (PAW, non-relativistic)
          Co.rel-pbesol-spn-kjpaw_psl.0.3.1.UPF (PAW, fully relativistic, for SOC)
      F:  F.oncvpsp.upf (ONCV norm-conserving)

--- STAGE 2: Self-Consistent Field + Hubbard U (2_scf/) ---
  Purpose: Iterative DFT+U+HP self-consistency to determine U.
  Code: pw.x (SCF) + hp.x (linear-response Hubbard U)
  Method: Antiferromagnetic, tot_magnetization=0, occupations='fixed'
  Cell: from vc-relax v1 output (ibrav=0, a≈4.6829 Å, c≈3.1655 Å)
  HP q-mesh: 2x2x3, find_atpert=4

  Iteration 1 (scf_0.50_1/):
    Input U: Co1=Co2=3.0 eV
    HP output U: Co1=Co2=6.0743 eV

  Iteration 2 (scf_0.50_2/):
    Input U: Co1=Co2=6.0743 eV
    HP output U: Co1≈6.2629 eV, Co2≈6.2625 eV

  CONCLUSION: U converged to ~6.26 eV for both Co sites.
  Note: scf_0.50/ and scf_0.99/ are earlier/alternative setups (checks).

================================================================================
PHYSICS CONTEXT
================================================================================

CoF2 is a rutile-structure antiferromagnet (AFM) that has been identified as
an altermagnet — a new class of magnetic material with zero net magnetization
but spin-split electronic bands due to crystal symmetry (not SOC).

Key physics:
  - Altermagnetism: Co1 (spin-up) and Co2 (spin-down) are related by a
    rotation (not inversion/translation), breaking time-reversal symmetry
    in k-space → spin-split bands even without SOC.
  - The AM phase is the ground state, lower than FM by ~21 meV/cell.
  - DFT+U is needed to correctly describe Co-3d correlations.
  - SOC + Wannier90 enables calculation of anomalous Hall conductivity,
    spin Hall conductivity, and other topological/transport properties.
  - The Wannier basis (Co-d + F-p) captures the relevant bands near E_F.
